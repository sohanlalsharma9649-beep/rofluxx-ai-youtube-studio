What this first release can and cannot do

Works now in supported browsers: local video preview, trim, aspect-ratio reframing, optional caption overlay, WebM export, topic-based SEO draft, PNG text thumbnail, Google OAuth connection and YouTube Unlisted upload.

Not included yet: automatic AI scene/highlight detection, automatic music selection, professional effects, live trend research, cloud storage, scheduled uploads, analytics dashboard or guaranteed monetization. Browser video rendering can be slow and may fail for very large files or on low-memory phones. Keep the page open while rendering/uploading.
